Glyphseed

Glyphseed grows a sigil from your words. Every character you type becomes a permanent mark in the composition — vowels are luminous nodes, consonants are radial blades, digits become polygons with that many sides, spaces drop concentric rings between words, and symbols sprout fractal twigs.

It is a visual cipher: the mapping from text to image is fully deterministic. The same phrase always grows the exact same image, pixel-for-pixel — no randomness, no clock, nothing stored. Change one letter and the whole sigil shifts. The image is a faithful signature of the message; the only way to reproduce or verify it is to type the same words into this same generator. The short code beside the art is the input's fingerprint — proof of that determinism.
